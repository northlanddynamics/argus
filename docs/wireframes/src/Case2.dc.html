What changes on the Case page, and why

  1. 1Properties as compact chips, with Undo.Four full-width selects take half the header, and every pick PATCHes immediately with no confirmation or undo — a slip turns a Case Critical. Chips show the value as a badge, save on pick and offer Undo in a toast. Status shows how long it's been in that Status.Now: case-layout.tsx, four NativeSelects with onChange → change.mutate.
  2. 2One primary action that follows the Status; the rest under “More”.“Close Case” is the only action and it's an outline button. In Progress → “Mark Resolved”; Resolved → “Close Case…”; Closed → “Reopen” (Admins). Export, Invite Guest, Link sit under More as they arrive.
  3. 3Header and tabs stay pinned; every tab carries a count or a signal.On long Timelines and Audit Logs the tabs scroll away. Evidence and Timeline have no counts; new Discussion messages and overdue Tasks have no signal.
  4. 4“Needs attention” at the top of the Overview.Overdue Tasks, @mentions of you and Tasks due soon, each one click from the item. Today the Overview is a Summary and a near-empty Details card.
  5. 5Show who is editing the Case Summary before you start.The glossary says it is edited by one person at a time, but the app only finds out at Save (409 → conflict banner). Presence (ADR 0005) can say “M. Chen is editing” up front. Drafts survive leaving the tab; the conflict view compares side by side.Now: overview.tsx, conflict banner after a refused save; draft lost on navigation.
  6. 6Latest Timeline Entries on the Overview.A reader landing on the Case sees the newest facts without switching tabs.
  7. 7Assignee and Case Roles together under People.Removing a Case Role is an instant ✕ with no confirm; it asks first when it would remove someone's access to a Restricted Case.Now: case-roles-panel.tsx, unassign on click.
  8. 8Restricted moves to an Access card with a confirm step.Today a checkbox in the header flips it instantly; if that removes your own access the page silently navigates to the list. The dialog lists who gains or loses access first.Now: case-layout.tsx, Checkbox → change.mutate; onSuccess navigates to “/” when the Case vanishes.
